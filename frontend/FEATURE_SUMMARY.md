# Ngam-je Platform Feature Summary

## 🚀 TLDR - Executive Summary

**Mission:** Ad-free, AI-powered marketplace focused on procurement, bounty hunting, and targeted sales

**Core Innovation:** Tinder-style matching where users swipe through compatible listings (not profiles), with AI-to-AI backend negotiation that pre-structures deals before human conversation begins.

**Revenue Model:** 3 streams
1. **Platform Subscriptions** (primary) - Monthly AI feature access
2. **Thread Boosts** - Community-funded AI for entire threads
3. **Escrow Commission** - 2-3% on completed transactions

**8 AI Features:**
1. **AI Search Assistant** (freemium)
2. **Agentic Mode** (premium) - AI operates platform on user's behalf
3. **FAQ + AI Moderation** (premium)
4. **AI Communication Agent** (CORE VALUE) - Compatibility matching + backend negotiation
5. **AI FAQ Correspondent** (premium) - Interactive chatbot for listings
6. **AI Listing Creation** (premium) - Auto-fill forms + market pricing
7. **AI Image Enhancement** (premium, buyers only) - Reference images for inspections
8. **AI Scheduling** (premium) - Calendly-style meetup coordination

**Monetization:** Paid listings create scarcity, prevent spam. Users pay for listing shelf-life extensions.

**Free vs Premium:** Free users get basic swipe/browse, premium gets AI-enhanced cards with compatibility scores and pre-negotiated terms.

**Implementation Priority:** Matching page → AI Communication Agent → Subscription gating → Additional AI features

## Core Platform Features

### Marketplace Foundation
- **Thread-based communities** for different product categories
- **Listing creation** (WTB/WTS) with rich media support
- **User profiles** with verification and reputation systems
- **Basic messaging** system between users
- **Search and filtering** capabilities

### Monetization System
- **Listing-based scarcity model** - paid listings prevent spam
- **Subscription tiers** with different listing quotas and shelf-life
- **Thread boost system** - community-funded AI features
- **Escrow commission** on successful transactions

## AI Feature Set (8 Core Features)

### 1. AI Search Assistant
**Free Tier:** Limited queries per month
**Paid Tier:** Higher usage limits
**Function:** Market research, price analysis, product guidance, buying advice

### 2. Agentic Mode (Premium Only)
**Function:** AI operates website on user's behalf
**Capabilities:**
- Creates personalized to-do lists
- Sets up automated tasks
- Works toward user-defined goals
- Monitors listings and market conditions

### 3. FAQ System with AI Moderation
**Free Tier:** Static FAQ creation and viewing
**Paid Tier:** AI-powered moderation and management
**Capabilities:**
- Auto-generate FAQ suggestions from similar threads
- Filter spam/troll tickets automatically
- Smart escalation to human moderators
- Ticket management dashboard

### 4. AI Communication Agent (CORE VALUE PROP)
**Function:** Analyzes buyer/seller compatibility and facilitates matching
**Capabilities:**
- Pre-match compatibility scoring
- AI-to-AI backend negotiation
- Smart introduction facilitation
- Deal probability analysis

### 5. AI FAQ Correspondent
**Function:** Interactive chatbot for product inquiries
**Capabilities:**
- Answers questions about specific listings
- Provides product information without reading static text
- Escalates complex questions to sellers
- Available 24/7 for immediate responses

### 6. AI-Assisted Listing Creation (Premium Only)
**Function:** AI populates listing forms using web-scraped market data
**Capabilities:**
- Real-time price chart population from multiple sources
- Auto-form filling from user descriptions/photos
- Market insights and pricing recommendations
- Listing optimization suggestions

**Free User Experience:**
- Grayed out price charts with "Get AI price analysis with Premium"
- Manual form entry only with upgrade prompts
- Clear value demonstration without giving features away

### 7. AI Image Enhancement (Premium Only, Buyers Only)
**Function:** Generate/enhance product inspection photos for buyers
**Use Case:** Buyer wants to show expected product condition
**Capabilities:**
- Reference image generation for condition standards
- Comparison visuals and quality examples
- Inspection guides and checklists
**Note:** Sellers must use actual product photos for authenticity

**Free User Experience:**
- Placeholder image gallery with upgrade prompts
- "Generate reference images with Premium" messaging
- Clear demonstration of missing AI visualization features

### 8. AI-Powered Scheduling (Premium Only)
**Function:** Intelligent meetup coordination for product inspections
**Capabilities:**
- Personal calendar integration and sync
- Smart scheduling with personalized availability questions
- Location optimization for convenient meetups
- Inspection planning with AI-generated checklists
**Restriction:** Only for physical meetups/inspections

**Free User Experience:**
- Basic calendar widget for manual scheduling only
- "AI-powered scheduling available" with feature descriptions
- Direct upgrade buttons showing specific premium benefits

## Matching System (Tinder-Style)

### Core Mechanics
- **Listing-based matching** (not profile-based)
- **Swipe interface** for reviewing potential matches
- **AI-enhanced cards** showing compatibility and insights (premium)
- **Backend AI negotiation** between matched listings

### Free vs Premium Experience

**Free Users:**
- Basic swipe cards with listing info only
- Manual message writing
- No AI insights or compatibility scores
- Standard marketplace experience

**Premium Users:**
- AI-enhanced swipe cards with:
  - Compatibility scores
  - Deal probability analysis
  - Conversation style previews
  - Pre-negotiated terms
- AI-crafted introduction messages
- Real-time negotiation coaching

### AI Negotiation Transparency
- **Default:** Clean results summary only
- **Optional:** Expandable negotiation details
- **Full Transparency:** Complete AI conversation log (on demand)
- **User Control:** Choose comfort level of detail

## Listing Lifecycle & Monetization

### Listing Tiers
**Free Users:** Cannot create listings (browse only)

**Basic Premium:**
- 2 listings per month
- 30-day shelf life each
- $1 to extend by 7 days

**Pro Premium:**
- 5 listings per month
- 60-day shelf life each
- $0.50 to extend by 7 days

**Enterprise:**
- 20 listings per month
- 90-day shelf life
- Bulk extension discounts

### Thread Boost System
- **Community-funded AI features** for threads
- **Boost levels** unlock different AI capabilities for all thread members
- **Dual acquisition:** From subscriptions (credits) or direct purchase
- **Viral growth:** Boosted threads attract more members

## Revenue Streams (Finalized)

### 1. Platform Subscription (Primary)
- Monthly recurring revenue from subscription tiers
- AI feature access and listing quotas
- Includes thread boost credits for community investment

### 2. Community Thread Boosts
- Direct purchases to enhance thread AI capabilities
- Subscriber credits can be spent on boosts
- Creates community investment and viral growth

### 3. Escrow-Based Commission
- 2-3% commission on successful transactions
- Only charged when deals complete through platform
- Covers payment processing and dispute resolution
